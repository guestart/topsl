FAQ

Quick answers

What exactly do I get per lesson?
Four deliverables in one WinRAR .zip: a video walkthrough (.mp4), a slide deck (.pdf), an at-a-glance infographic (.png), and a self-contained podcast app with audio (.html + .m4a). Download once, study offline, keep forever.
What’s in the free tier?
20 lessons — at least two from every discipline. Free lessons are not samples with the good parts removed: they’re complete lessons with all four formats, linked directly from this site. No sign-up required.
What level is this for?
Beginner to intermediate Oracle DBAs and developers. You should be comfortable with basic SQL and the Oracle environment; each lesson explains its own concepts from first principles.
How do I get the paid lessons?
Pick a discipline and complete the one-time purchase on Gumroad ($5–12 depending on the discipline). You then download that discipline’s lesson zips straight to your machine.
Do I need a running Oracle database to follow along?
Recommended, not required. A free Oracle Developer Day / Express instance is enough to reproduce the examples. If you can’t spin one up, the slide decks and infographics carry the full argument on their own.
Which Oracle versions do the lessons cover?
The techniques apply across 11gR2 → 19c. Modern syntax shown in the lessons (such as FETCH FIRST) requires 12c+; legacy equivalents are always explained alongside.
